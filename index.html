Your notes, on your computer.

qnote is a desktop app for writing and linking Markdown notes. Notes are plain files in a folder you choose, and they can sync between your computers through cloud storage you already own.

Windows · macOS · Linux
📝

Plain Markdown files

Every note is a .md file in a folder on your disk. Open it with any editor, back it up any way you like.

🔗

Linked thinking

[[Wikilinks]], backlinks, tags, properties and an outline turn notes into a connected knowledge base.

✨

Live preview editor

Headings, tasks, callouts, math and embedded images render as you type.

🔎

Fast search

Search by words, phrases, paths, tags, properties or regular expressions; jump anywhere with the quick switcher.

☁️

Sync with your cloud

Keep a vault in sync across computers through your own cloud storage or any synced folder. Edits on different lines merge automatically.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no qnote server, no analytics. Your notes go only where you send them.

How sync works

Sync is optional. qnote copies your vault straight to storage you own and choose: a cloud service you connect, or any folder (for example one kept in sync by OneDrive or Dropbox for desktop, a USB drive or a network share). There is no qnote server and no qnote account. More cloud services will be added over time.

Google Drive

When you click Connect, you sign in with Google and qnote asks for one permission: access to the files that qnote itself creates in your Drive (the drive.file scope). qnote cannot see or change anything else in your Drive.

  • qnote creates a folder such as qnote/My vault in your Drive and keeps a copy of your vault there.
  • Files travel directly between your computer and Google. There is no qnote server in between.
  • Your sign-in is stored encrypted on your computer. Disconnect at any time from qnote, or from your Google account permissions.
